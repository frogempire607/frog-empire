Mindset & Performance Coaching

Building Champions on the Mat. Preparing Them for Life.

Wrestling is one of life’s greatest training grounds. It builds confidence, discipline, resilience, leadership, and character that last long after an athlete leaves the mat.

I’m Tyler Lehmann, a three-time high school state champion, former U.S. World Team member, Pan American champion in freestyle and Greco-Roman wrestling, and former collegiate coach. I’ve competed at the highest levels and know physical preparation is only part of success. Your ability to handle pressure, respond to adversity, and trust yourself often makes the difference.

Through PEAK Mindset & Performance, I help wrestlers and other athletes compete with confidence, overcome self-doubt, recover from setbacks, and build championship habits. Athletes leave with practical mental skills they can use in competition, school, work, and life.

As a husband and father of three, I know success means more than trophies. We want young people to know their worth, embrace challenges, take responsibility, and pursue their potential with courage.

I also coach collegiate and professional athletes, entrepreneurs, parents, and others committed to personal growth. The goal stays the same: build confidence, purpose, discipline, and the mindset to perform when it matters.

My mission is bigger than creating better wrestlers. I help develop stronger people, capable leaders, and champions in life.
